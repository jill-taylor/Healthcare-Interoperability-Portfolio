# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish the organizational and technical context for the Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS) current-state assessment.

The assessment will describe the health system's geographic footprint, facilities, clinical environment, technology environment, and selected network-connected infusion-pump environment.

This document provides the context needed to interpret the subsequent device, interface, architecture, clinical workflow, and cybersecurity assessments.

The assessment is limited to the selected infusion-pump environment and its associated operational and technical dependencies. It does not attempt to document every technology, system, or clinical service within HIHS.
