# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish the organizational, clinical, operational, and technical context for the Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS) current-state assessment.

The assessment will describe the health system's geographic footprint, facilities, clinical environment, technology environment, and selected legacy infusion-pump environment, including network-connected and non-network-connected devices and relevant workflow, information, and communication dependencies.

This document provides the context needed to interpret the subsequent device, interface, architecture, clinical workflow, usability, communication, and cybersecurity assessments.

The assessment is limited to the selected legacy infusion-pump environment and the clinical, operational, information, communication, cybersecurity, and technical dependencies directly relevant to that environment. It does not attempt to document every technology, system, workflow, or clinical service within HIHS.
