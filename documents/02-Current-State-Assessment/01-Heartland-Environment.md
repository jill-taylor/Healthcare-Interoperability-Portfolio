# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish the organizational, clinical, operational, and technical context for the Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS) current-state assessment.

The assessment will describe the health system's geographic footprint, facilities, clinical environment, technology environment, and selected network-connected infusion-pump environment, including relevant workflow, information, and communication dependencies.

This document provides the context needed to interpret the subsequent device, interface, architecture, clinical workflow, usability, communication, and cybersecurity assessments.

The assessment is limited to the selected infusion-pump environment and its associated clinical, operational, and technical dependencies. It does not attempt to document every technology, system, workflow, or clinical service within HIHS.
