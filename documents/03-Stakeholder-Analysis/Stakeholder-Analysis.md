# Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS)
## Stakeholder Analysis

### Legacy Infusion Pump Modernization Assessment

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify the individuals, teams, leadership groups, and external parties whose knowledge, responsibilities, information needs, or decisions may affect the Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS) Legacy Infusion Pump Modernization Assessment.

The analysis establishes:

- Stakeholder roles and responsibilities
- Primary interests and concerns
- Information and communication needs
- Sources of subject-matter expertise
- Stakeholder influence and decision roles
- Areas of accountability and coordination
- Stakeholder risks and dependencies
- Engagement considerations for current-state discovery

Stakeholder roles and responsibilities are role-based and fictional for this portfolio assessment.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis Approach

The assessment considers each stakeholder group's relationship to the current environment rather than organizational hierarchy alone.

The analysis considers:

- Responsibilities within the current environment
- Areas of interest or concern
- Information required to support assessment activities
- Information or evidence contributed by the stakeholder
- Role in validation or subject-matter review
- Influence on assessment outcomes or subsequent decisions
- Communication and coordination needs
- Dependencies that may affect discovery or validation

Stakeholder input may represent reported information, professional judgment, or subject-matter expertise. Such information should be distinguished from independently validated evidence where appropriate.

Conflicting or incomplete stakeholder information should be documented as an assumption, dependency, unresolved question, or area requiring further validation rather than silently reconciled.

## 5. Stakeholder Roles and Responsibilities

### Executive Leadership

Executive Leadership provides organizational direction and reviews consolidated assessment findings.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Reviewing significant risks and gaps
- Understanding dependencies and constraints
- Clarifying organizational priorities
- Reviewing decision-readiness considerations
- Providing direction for subsequent project phases

Future modernization, procurement, and implementation decisions remain outside the scope of this assessment.

### Clinical Leadership

Clinical Leadership provides clinical perspective regarding:

- Clinical priorities
- Workflow dependencies
- Device criticality
- Patient-safety considerations
- Clinical continuity
- Operational impacts

Clinical Leadership also supports validation of clinical findings and workflow considerations.

### Clinical Users

Clinical users provide practitioner-level information regarding:

- Actual workflow
- Device use
- Information needs
- Usability considerations
- Workarounds
- Operational constraints
- Clinical continuity concerns

Clinical-user input should be documented as stakeholder-reported information unless independently validated.

### Clinical Engineering / Biomedical Engineering

Clinical Engineering / Biomedical Engineering provides subject-matter expertise related to:

- Device inventory
- Device characteristics
- Lifecycle position
- Maintenance
- Vendor support
- Device dependencies
- Operational constraints

### IT Infrastructure / Networking

IT Infrastructure / Networking provides information regarding:

- Network connectivity
- Infrastructure relationships
- Monitoring
- Network dependencies
- Security boundaries
- Operational support

### Integration / Interface Team

The Integration / Interface Team provides information regarding:

- Interfaces
- Information flows
- System relationships
- Integration dependencies
- Ownership
- Monitoring
- Operational dependencies

### Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity provides subject-matter expertise regarding:

- Device connectivity and exposure
- Access controls
- Monitoring
- Lifecycle-related security considerations
- Security dependencies
- Security update considerations
- Areas requiring further investigation

### EHR / Clinical Systems Team

The EHR / Clinical Systems Team provides information regarding:

- Clinical system relationships
- Information exchange
- Workflow dependencies
- Integration considerations
- Clinical-system context

### Vendor / Manufacturer Representatives

Vendor or manufacturer representatives may provide:

- Device capabilities
- Product lifecycle information
- Support status
- Technical documentation
- Vendor-specific dependencies
- Interface or connectivity information
- Security-related information

Vendor-provided information should be identified as vendor-reported information unless independently validated.

### Technical Project Manager / Assessment Team

The Technical Project Manager coordinates the assessment and supports:

- Assessment planning
- Stakeholder coordination
- Evidence tracking
- Risk and dependency tracking
- Assumption management
- Documentation
- Communication
- Findings development
- Decision-readiness support

The Technical Project Manager does not serve as the technical, clinical, cybersecurity, networking, integration, or medical-device authority.

## 7. Stakeholder Engagement Approach

Stakeholder engagement may include:

- Interviews
- Working sessions
- Document review
- Technical validation
- Workflow review
- Device or inventory validation
- Interface and information-flow review
- Cybersecurity review
- Vendor information requests
- Findings review and clarification

Engagement should focus on understanding and validating the current state rather than developing or approving future-state solutions.

### Engagement Priorities

Clinical engagement should focus on workflow, usability, information needs, device criticality, and continuity.

Technical engagement should focus on device characteristics, connectivity, interfaces, information flows, infrastructure, monitoring, and operational dependencies.

Cybersecurity engagement should focus on available security information, exposure, access, monitoring, lifecycle-related concerns, and areas requiring additional investigation.

Vendor engagement should focus on product capabilities, lifecycle, support, documentation, and unresolved technical questions.

Leadership engagement should focus on consolidated findings, significant risks and dependencies, unresolved questions, and decision-readiness.

## 8. Stakeholder Risks and Dependencies

Potential stakeholder-related risks and dependencies include:

- Incomplete stakeholder participation
- Conflicting stakeholder information
- Unclear ownership of devices, systems, interfaces, or processes
- Limited or outdated documentation
- Dependence on vendor-provided information
- Facility-specific differences
- Competing stakeholder priorities
- Limited subject-matter expert availability
- Unresolved assumptions
- Incomplete validation of reported information

These risks may affect:

- Evidence quality
- Assessment findings
- Validation activities
- Risk and gap identification
- Assessment timelines
- Decision readiness

Unresolved stakeholder dependencies should remain visible as open questions, assumptions, risks, or areas requiring further investigation.
