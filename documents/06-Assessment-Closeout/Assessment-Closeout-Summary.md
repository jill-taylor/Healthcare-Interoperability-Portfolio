# Assessment Closeout Summary

## Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS)
### Legacy Infusion Pump Modernization Assessment

**Assessment Phase:** Current-State Discovery and Decision Readiness  
**Project Type:** Assessment and Planning  
**Status:** Portfolio Artifact

---

## 1. Purpose

This document summarizes the completion of the Heartland Integrated Health System (HIHS) Legacy Infusion Pump Modernization Assessment and formally closes the current-state discovery and decision-readiness phase.

The assessment was designed to establish a structured understanding of the legacy infusion-pump environment, including devices, clinical workflows, information flows, integration dependencies, cybersecurity considerations, lifecycle conditions, stakeholders, risks, and evidence gaps.

The assessment was conducted as a discovery and planning effort. It did not include procurement, implementation, production configuration, future-state engineering, or selection of a modernization solution.

---

## 2. Assessment Objectives

The assessment objectives were to:

- Establish a current-state device inventory.
- Understand relevant clinical workflows and operational dependencies.
- Identify connectivity, information-flow, and integration dependencies.
- Assess cybersecurity and operational considerations.
- Identify lifecycle and vendor-support conditions.
- Identify current-state risks, gaps, assumptions, and evidence limitations.
- Identify stakeholders and decision roles.
- Determine whether sufficient information exists to support responsible next-phase planning.
- Document areas requiring additional investigation.

---

## 3. Assessment Activities Completed

The assessment produced and reviewed the following major artifacts:

- Project Charter
- Discovery Plan
- Device Inventory
- Stakeholder Analysis
- Clinical Workflow Assessment
- Interface Inventory
- Current-State Architecture
- Cybersecurity Assessment
- Current-State Findings
- Risk and Gap Assessment
- Decision-Readiness Findings
- QGIS-based visualization activities

These artifacts collectively document the current-state discovery effort and provide traceability among evidence, findings, risks, gaps, stakeholder considerations, and decision-readiness.

---

## 4. Assessment Findings

The assessment established a structured baseline of the HIHS legacy infusion-pump environment.

Key areas identified include:

- A preliminary population of approximately 480 infusion pumps across the identified HIHS facilities.
- Variation in device age, connectivity, integration status, lifecycle position, and vendor-support conditions.
- Dependencies among infusion pumps, network infrastructure, integration components, enterprise clinical systems, monitoring capabilities, asset records, identity and access management, and vendor support.
- Clinical workflow and information-flow dependencies requiring continued validation.
- Cybersecurity and operational considerations associated with device lifecycle, connectivity, access, monitoring, vendor dependencies, and continuity.
- Facility-specific differences and evidence gaps that may affect consistency of the current-state understanding.
- Stakeholder, ownership, documentation, and validation dependencies requiring attention during subsequent planning.

The findings are documented in greater detail within the individual assessment artifacts.

---

## 5. Risks, Gaps, and Unresolved Information

The assessment identified risks and gaps associated with:

- Device and lifecycle information
- Interface and integration dependencies
- Clinical workflow validation
- Cybersecurity visibility and controls
- Monitoring and operational support
- Facility-specific variation
- Stakeholder participation and ownership
- Incomplete or conflicting documentation
- Unresolved assumptions and evidence gaps

The assessment distinguishes identified conditions from potential risks, stakeholder-reported information, assumptions, and items requiring further investigation.

Unresolved evidence gaps have not been treated as confirmed findings.

---

## 6. Decision Readiness

The assessment determined that current-state discovery is sufficiently developed to identify next-phase information and validation needs.

However, additional investigation is required before major modernization, procurement, architecture, or implementation decisions can be responsibly made.

The assessment therefore supports **continued planning and targeted validation**, rather than authorization of a modernization solution.

Specific decision-readiness considerations are documented in the Decision-Readiness Findings artifact.

---

## 7. Assessment Limitations

This assessment is a fictional portfolio case study representing a current-state discovery engagement.

The assessment does not constitute:

- A production healthcare assessment
- A clinical safety determination
- A cybersecurity certification or authorization
- A regulatory or compliance determination
- A formal enterprise risk assessment
- A vendor evaluation
- A procurement recommendation
- A future-state architecture
- An implementation plan
- A production configuration

Where information was unavailable or could not be independently validated, the assessment identifies the limitation or need for further investigation.

---

## 8. Assessment Outcome

The current-state discovery and decision-readiness objectives have been completed within the defined portfolio scope.

The assessment provides HIHS with:

- A structured current-state baseline
- Documented clinical and operational dependencies
- Identified integration and information-flow dependencies
- Identified cybersecurity and lifecycle considerations
- Documented stakeholder and ownership considerations
- Consolidated risks and gaps
- Identified evidence and validation needs
- A decision-readiness determination for the next phase

The assessment does not recommend a specific modernization solution.

Instead, it establishes the information and validation foundation required for responsible future planning.

---

## 9. Transition to the Next Phase

Any subsequent modernization planning should begin by reviewing the findings, risks, gaps, assumptions, and evidence requirements documented during this assessment.

Before major decisions are made, HIHS should validate outstanding device, lifecycle, workflow, interface, cybersecurity, operational, and stakeholder information identified as requiring further investigation.

Future-state planning should build upon validated current-state evidence rather than assume that unresolved conditions have been resolved.

---

## 10. Closeout Statement

The **Heartland Integrated Health System Legacy Infusion Pump Modernization Assessment** is considered complete for the defined current-state discovery and decision-readiness scope.

The assessment has established a documented foundation for future planning while preserving unresolved questions and evidence limitations for subsequent investigation.

The engagement closes with the following guiding principle:

> **Understand Before Recommending.**
