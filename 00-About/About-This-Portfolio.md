# About This Portfolio

Welcome to my Healthcare Interoperability Portfolio.

For more than twenty years, I have supported complex, mission-critical technical programs involving military aircraft maintenance documentation, structured authoring, cloud technologies, APIs, and machine learning platforms. Throughout my career, I have collaborated with engineers, program managers, quality organizations, and technical teams to develop controlled documentation supporting highly regulated environments.

As I expand my expertise into healthcare interoperability, I wanted to do more than complete certifications. I wanted to demonstrate how systems thinking, technical documentation, project planning, and engineering discipline can be applied to modern healthcare technology initiatives.

This portfolio contains a collection of realistic healthcare interoperability projects developed to showcase my approach to technical project management, enterprise integration, technical architecture, implementation planning, and project governance. Each project is based on established healthcare standards and common modernization challenges faced by hospitals and health systems.

These portfolio projects are professional learning engagements designed to demonstrate analysis, planning, communication, and technical leadership. While they are not production implementations, they reflect industry practices and illustrate how I approach complex systems, stakeholder collaboration, and technology modernization.

My goal is to continue building expertise in healthcare interoperability while leveraging the systems engineering mindset, structured documentation, and project management practices developed throughout my career.

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. I hope these projects provide insight into how I approach solving complex technical challenges through thoughtful planning, clear communication, and disciplined execution.
